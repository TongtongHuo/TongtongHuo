<h3>📖 Biography</h3>
<p>
I am currently a Lecturer and the Director of the Teaching and Research Office of the Department of Electronic Information at the <b>School of Electronic Information, Wuhan University of Science and Technology (WUST)</b>.
</p>
<p>
My research centers on <b>AI-aided Diagnosis and Treatment in Orthopedics</b> (骨科人工智能辅助诊断与治疗), with specific focuses on <b>Pediatric Medical Image Recognition</b> (儿童影像识别), <b>Cross-modal Transfer Learning</b> (跨模态迁移学习), and algorithmic research for <b>Clinical Decision Support Systems</b> (临床决策支持系统). I have established a technical system for standardized medical image processing and 3D reconstruction recognition, with extensive work in computer vision-aided diagnosis and multi-modal data fusion.
</p>
<p>
To date, I have published over <b>20 SCI papers</b>, including 10+ papers as First/Co-first Author in journals such as <i>International Journal of Surgery</i>, <i>Radiology Advances</i>, <i>iScience</i>, and <i>Frontiers in Oncology</i>. My work includes cross-modal AI diagnostic models for pediatric hip fractures and transfer learning studies on multicenter adult tibial plateau fractures. I hold <b>7 authorized invention patents</b> (including 2 WIPO international patents).
</p>
